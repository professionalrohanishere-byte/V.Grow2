About V.Grow

I started this because most advice online is written by people who've already arrived.

V.Grow began as a private notes app on my phone — half-finished thoughts about what was actually working in my day-to-day life, and what wasn't. At some point I realized most of what I was reading online about growth, health, and relationships was written from the finish line, by people who'd forgotten what the starting line felt like.

So I decided to write from where I actually am. That's the whole premise of this site: I try something, I pay attention to what happens, and I write it down honestly — the parts that worked and the parts that didn't.

"Growth isn't a straight line, and I'm not interested in pretending mine is."

What you'll find here

Articles on the things I'm genuinely working through — building better habits, navigating relationships, figuring out travel on a real budget, and the small daily decisions that add up over time. Nothing here is written by a committee of experts. It's written by one person, paying close attention, and telling you the truth about it.

What I won't do

I won't tell you I've got it all figured out, because I don't. I won't dress up a guess as a guarantee. And if something didn't work for me, I'll say so instead of quietly leaving it out.

A quick note on trust

Some links on this site are affiliate links, and some pages carry ads — that's what keeps V.Grow running without a paywall. I only recommend things I'd genuinely use myself. You can read the full details on the Privacy Policy page.

Thanks for reading. If something here helps, even a little, it was worth writing.
