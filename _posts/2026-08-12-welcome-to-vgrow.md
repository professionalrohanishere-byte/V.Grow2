title: "Welcome to V.Grow — Why I'm Writing This in Public"
---

I've started and abandoned a dozen journals over the years. This one's different because it's not private — and honestly, that's the point.

When advice is written for no one in particular, it tends to stay vague. When it's written for someone actually reading it, it has to hold up. That's what I'm going for here: articles specific enough that they might actually help.

Here's what to expect from V.Grow going forward — practical write-ups, drawn from things I'm genuinely working through, published as I figure them out rather than after I've got it all neatly solved.

If you're here, thanks for showing up early. More soon.
